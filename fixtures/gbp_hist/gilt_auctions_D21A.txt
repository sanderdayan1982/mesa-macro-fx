Note:   1. Conventional gilts are auctioned on a multiple price basis, while index-linked gilts are auctioned on a uniform price basis. As a result, for index-linked gilt auctions

        i.e. it is the price excluding any inflation uplift. Tail figures for conventional gilt auctions from November 1998 have been calculated from unrounded yields.

        computed using a 3% inflation assumption, while for index-linked gilts with a 3-month indexation lag the yield does not depend on an inflation assumption.

        6. PAOF is the Post Auction Option Facility which was introduced at outright gilt auctions from 1 June 2009. Under the option, successful bidders can purchase (at the
